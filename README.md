## A Simple Workflow for Beginners

If you are new to AI-assisted social media management, start with this process:

1. **Choose an objective.** Decide whether you want to educate an audience, promote a product, share updates, or build brand awareness.
2. **Define your audience.** Identify who the content is for and what they care about.
3. **Develop a content idea.** Start with one useful topic instead of trying to manage every channel at once.
4. **Prepare platform-appropriate content.** Adapt the format and message to each platform's audience and requirements.
5. **Review before publishing.** Check accuracy, tone, links, media, and platform-specific requirements.
6. **Evaluate the result.** Review meaningful engagement and business outcomes, then improve the next round of content.

AI can help accelerate parts of this process, but human judgment remains important for accuracy, originality, and audience relevance.
